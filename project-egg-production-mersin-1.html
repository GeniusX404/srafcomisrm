Project Overview

This comprehensive egg production facility in Mersin, Turkey, represents a significant milestone in modern poultry farming. The 5,000 square meter facility was designed to house 50,000 laying hens with state-of-the-art equipment and automation systems.

The project included complete construction, electrical installation, and integration of all farm technology systems. Our team worked closely with the client to ensure optimal bird welfare, production efficiency, and environmental sustainability.

Key Features & Specifications

  • 50,000 laying hens capacity
  • Complete cage system installation
  • Automated feeding systems
  • Advanced drinking systems
  • Climate control and ventilation
  • Egg collection and sorting systems
  • Manure removal systems
  • Lighting control systems
  • Security and monitoring systems
  • Quality control integration

Technical Specifications

Building Dimensions: 100m x 50m x 4.5m height
Cage System: Multi-tier enriched cages
Feeding System: Chain feeding with automatic distribution
Drinking System: Nipple drinking system with pressure control
Climate Control: Automated temperature and humidity control
Ventilation: Variable speed fans with air quality monitoring
Egg Collection: Automated collection and sorting system
Electrical System: Complete electrical installation with backup power

Project Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: The project required integration of multiple complex systems while maintaining optimal bird welfare standards.

Solution: Our engineering team developed a comprehensive system integration plan that ensured seamless operation of all equipment while maintaining the highest standards of animal welfare and production efficiency.

Results & Performance

The facility has been operating successfully since completion, achieving:

  • 95%+ egg production efficiency
  • Reduced labor costs by 60% through automation
  • Improved egg quality and consistency
  • Enhanced bird welfare and health
  • Reduced environmental impact through efficient systems
