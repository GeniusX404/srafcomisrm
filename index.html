F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our poultry equipment and farm technology services

  • What types of poultry equipment do you provide?

    We provide comprehensive poultry equipment including cage systems, feeding equipment, drinking systems, egg collection systems, climate control systems, and complete automation solutions for modern poultry farming operations.

  • Do you provide international services?

    Yes, we provide services internationally. We have successfully completed projects in Turkey, Qatar, and other countries. Our team can handle international projects with local support and compliance with regional standards.

  • What is the typical project timeline?

    Project timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity. A typical poultry facility project takes 6-12 months from design to commissioning. We provide detailed project schedules during the consultation phase.

  • Do you offer financing options?

    We work with various financing partners and can assist with Turkish Ministry of Industry and European Union TKDK funding programs. Our team can help you explore available financing options for your project.

  • What kind of support do you provide after project completion?

    We provide comprehensive post-project support including training for your team, preventive maintenance programs, 24/7 technical support, spare parts supply, and performance optimization services to ensure continuous operation.
