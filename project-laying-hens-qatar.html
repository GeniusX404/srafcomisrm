International Project Overview

This landmark project in Al-Khor, Qatar, represents our most significant international achievement. The 12,500 square meter facility was designed to house 125,000 laying hens with complete turnkey solutions, advanced automation, and climate adaptation systems specifically designed for the Middle Eastern environment.

The project required extensive international coordination, compliance with local regulations, and adaptation of our technology to the unique climate and operational requirements of Qatar. Our team successfully delivered a world-class facility that exceeds international standards.

International Project Challenges

Climate Adaptation: The extreme desert climate of Qatar required specialized cooling systems and climate control solutions to maintain optimal conditions for laying hens.

Local Compliance: Ensuring compliance with Qatari regulations and international standards while maintaining our high-quality standards.

Logistics & Coordination: Managing international logistics, local partnerships, and coordination across different time zones and cultures.

Innovation & Technology

This project incorporates cutting-edge technology including IoT-based monitoring, predictive analytics, advanced climate control systems, and energy-efficient operations designed specifically for the Middle Eastern market.
