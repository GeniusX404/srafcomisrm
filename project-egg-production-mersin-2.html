Project Overview

This large-scale egg production facility in Mersin represents one of our most ambitious projects to date. The 8,500 square meter facility was designed to house 85,000 laying hens with cutting-edge multi-tier cage systems and comprehensive automation.

The project required extensive planning and coordination to integrate multiple complex systems while maintaining optimal bird welfare and production efficiency. Our team successfully delivered a facility that exceeds industry standards for automation and environmental control.

Advanced Features & Specifications

  • 85,000 laying hens capacity

  • Advanced automation systems
  • Environmental control systems

  • Automated feeding systems
  • Smart drinking systems
  • Egg collection automation
  • Manure management systems
  • Quality monitoring systems

Technical Specifications

Building Dimensions: 170m x 50m x 5.5m height
Cage System: 4-tier enriched cage systems
Feeding System: Automated chain feeding with precision control
Drinking System: Nipple drinking with pressure monitoring
Climate Control: Zone-based temperature and humidity control
Ventilation: Variable speed fans with air quality sensors
Egg Collection: Automated collection, sorting, and packaging
Monitoring: Real-time performance monitoring and alerts

Innovation & Technology

This facility incorporates the latest innovations in poultry farming technology, including IoT-based monitoring systems, predictive maintenance capabilities, and advanced data analytics for optimal performance management.

Environmental Considerations

The facility was designed with environmental sustainability in mind, featuring energy-efficient systems, waste management solutions, and reduced carbon footprint through optimized operations.

Results & Performance

Since completion, the facility has achieved exceptional results:

  • 97%+ egg production efficiency
  • Reduced labor costs by 70% through automation
  • Improved egg quality and consistency
  • Enhanced bird welfare and health metrics
  • Significant reduction in environmental impact
  • ROI achieved within 3 years of operation
