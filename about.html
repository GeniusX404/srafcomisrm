Rafco Farm Tech - Your Trusted Partner in Farm Technology

With over 12 years of experience in the agricultural industry, Rafco Farm Tech has established itself as a leading provider of poultry equipment and farm technology solutions. We specialize in delivering comprehensive livestock projects with complete construction, electrical, and industrial works.

Our commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred choice for farm technology solutions across Turkey and international markets. We combine traditional agricultural expertise with cutting-edge technology to deliver solutions that maximize productivity and efficiency.

Livestock Projects

Complete livestock project development with construction, electrical, and industrial works for all types of agricultural facilities

Project Studies

Comprehensive feasibility studies, construction licensing, and project planning services for animal and agricultural production

Quality Equipment

High-quality poultry equipment including cage systems, feeding equipment, and automated solutions for modern farming

Project Support

Expert support for projects backed by Turkish Ministry of Industry and European Union TKDK funding programs
