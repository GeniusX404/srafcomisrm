Feed Plant Project Overview

This comprehensive feed production facility in Tartous, Turkey, represents our expertise in agricultural feed processing technology. The 900 square meter facility was designed to produce high-quality feed with a capacity of 10 tons per hour, featuring automated mixing, pelleting, and quality control systems.

The project required specialized engineering for feed processing equipment, quality control systems, and automated operations. Our team successfully delivered a facility that meets international standards for feed production and quality assurance.

Feed Production Process

The facility features a complete feed production process including ingredient receiving, storage, automated mixing, pelleting, cooling, quality control testing, and automated packaging. Each step is monitored and controlled for optimal quality and efficiency.

Quality Assurance

The facility includes comprehensive quality control systems with automated sampling, testing equipment for nutritional analysis, and quality monitoring throughout the production process to ensure consistent, high-quality feed products.

Results & Performance

The feed plant has achieved excellent results since commissioning:

  • Consistent 10 tons/hour production capacity
  • 99.5% quality compliance rate
  • Reduced production costs by 25%
  • Improved feed quality and consistency
  • Automated operations with minimal manual intervention
  • Full traceability of ingredients and production batches
  • ROI achieved within 2 years of operation
